Install Magic Games

Add Magic Games to your home screen — it opens full-screen, just like a real app, and works offline.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome.
  2. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Tap Install — the 🪄 icon appears on your home screen.

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow, at the bottom).
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap Add — the 🪄 icon appears on your home screen.
